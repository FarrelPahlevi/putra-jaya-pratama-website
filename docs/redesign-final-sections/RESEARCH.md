No reference's animation quality is claimed from a still image. Unless explicitly noted, live hover, full scroll choreography, keyboard accessibility and production speed are **unverified**. Performance statements are implementation implications, not measured Lighthouse/Web Vitals scores. Mobile statements mean captured at 390px, not a complete responsive audit.

## Decisions carried into concepts

1. Four real photos, not four claimed named projects. Archive identifiers remain honest; no location/client names are added.
2. A static editorial composition is the recommended default. An immersive panorama and a manually controlled filmstrip are distinct working alternatives.
3. Gallery images open in a keyboard-accessible native dialog. The route `/galeri-proyek` remains obvious.
4. Dark consultation joins the footer through colour continuity and one precise divider. Orange is concentrated on the enquiry action.
5. No scrolling text, autoplay, scroll-jacking, WebGL, new dependencies, hidden text reveals or invented stats.
6. Responsive images, explicit dimensions, lazy secondary photos and a motion-free reduced-motion state are required.
