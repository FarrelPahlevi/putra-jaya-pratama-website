# Three directions for one final sequence

The brand stays Minimal Industrial: Barlow, warm paper, charcoal and pumpkin. The variation is composition and exploration, not a new company identity. These are prototypes of the final three sections only. The existing homepage is the comparison source.

## A. Industrial Editorial — recommended

**Idea:** a small, confidently edited field record. Large plain-language type shares a horizontal heading row with a concise explanation. One broad photograph anchors the gallery, with two smaller process views beside it. A fourth photograph is available through the archive viewer. Image controls are explicit, not hover-only.

**Sequence:** light photographic spread, then a dark invitation with the heading on the left and a short practical preparation ledger on the right. The footer continues the charcoal ground, separated by a thin rule. There is one primary enquiry action; the footer enquiry stays a quiet text link.

**Typography:** Barlow Semi Condensed gives the headline industrial character; Barlow body remains 16px or larger in the new sections. Contact numbers have greater weight than secondary social contact. No massive decorative wordmark.

**Motion:** the photo composition settles once by a small transform/opacity change while content remains visible. Image hover scales very slightly within its stable frame. Arrow feedback and focus rings identify controls. Reduced motion cancels settling and transforms.

**Mobile:** heading and explanation stack, dominant picture remains large, smaller process pictures become a two-column strip, consultation becomes a single column. Footer retains readable existing functions and safe WhatsApp clearance. No fixed section height.

**Advantages:** all three stages are visible through normal scrolling; evidence and conversion have clear hierarchy; strongest fit to real PJP photographs. **Trade-off:** less theatrical than a motion-led agency site; the photography must carry the quality.

## B. Architectural Portfolio

**Idea:** a panoramic field view. A wide, full-bleed-within-container photo leads immediately below a quieter left-aligned title; a small selected-photo control rail changes that stage. This is one focused visual at a time rather than A's simultaneous composition.

**Sequence:** the panoramic stage closes with the archive route. Consultation uses a split text/action layout on dark, with more restrained headline scale. Same compact footer. The dark ground, gutter and typography bind the sequence.

**Motion:** changing images gets a short opacity settle, no automatic slideshow or cross-page scroll choreography. Stage dimensions are fixed by aspect ratio. **Mobile:** a taller image crop and vertically wrapping selector, full keyboard and touch access.

**Advantages:** largest individual photographs; strong architectural confidence; low visual noise. **Trade-off:** some process evidence is one interaction away; selected-state controls need clear labels; mobile crops require care.

## C. Field Filmstrip

**Idea:** a manually explored contact sheet of road construction. Four large square-edged frames sit on a native horizontal rail, alternating process and finished-surface views. Previous/next controls supplement ordinary touch and trackpad scrolling; every frame can be opened directly.

**Sequence:** a more compact heading and rail, followed by a high-impact dark enquiry row. Footer remains the compact closure. The rail's edge hint suggests more photography without a carousel timer.

**Motion:** native scrolling with optional smooth button movement; there is no wheel interception or scroll-jacking. **Mobile:** a wide first frame and a visible sliver of the next; controls remain 44px, keyboard can reach each image.

**Advantages:** active exploration and a distinctive editorial strip; multiple photos available without a long page. **Trade-off:** horizontal discovery is less obvious than A; content can sit outside the first viewport; needs stronger controls and overflow containment.

## Evaluation

Scores are design judgement, not measured user-test results; 5 is strongest.

| Visual quality | 5 | 5 | 4 |
| PJP brand fit | 5 | 5 | 4 |
| Evidence visible immediately | 5 | 4 | 4 |
| Accessible normal-flow UX | 5 | 4 | 4 |
| Performance simplicity | 5 | 5 | 4 |
| Distinctive interaction | 4 | 4 | 5 |

**Select A.** It communicates real work before interaction, handles mixed photo orientations and gives consultation a clear next step. B contributes restraint and image scale; C remains a useful comparison, rather than adding its horizontal rail to A. Combining all mechanisms would fragment the sequence.

The isolated React prototype exposes all three compositions through clearly separated review controls. A is the initial state. Concept selection is delegated to the designer by the user's brief; prototype evaluation is still required before any integration into the approved homepage.

**Design Read:** final construction-company sections for prospective PJP clients; Minimal Industrial; ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1. Strong photography/type, different functional densities and quiet purposeful feedback.
